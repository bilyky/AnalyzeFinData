## Behaviour change to expect (fix 1)

- **What changes:** under BALANCED (20% buffer) and DEFENSIVE (50%), expansion now needs cash above 35% or
  65% respectively.
- **Effect today:** the game holds 10 positions against a base of 5 (BALANCED) or 3 (DEFENSIVE). So it makes
  **no new buys** until holdings fall below the base or cash rises above the buffer plus 15%.
- **Scale-ins stop too:** the pyramiding pass runs only when a slot is free (`aether/scenario/steps.py`,
  `available_slots > 0`). Before, expansion left a slot free whenever cash was above 15%, so scale-ins ran.
- **After the Groq patch:** the AI exit second opinion (R&D #14) gets answers again. Until now its calls fell
  through to Gemini's 503s. With answers, an AI HOLD can turn a momentum SELL of a winner more than 1 ATR in
  profit into HOLD. Stop exits and losers always sell. This is the designed behaviour coming back, not a new rule.
- **Unchanged:** in a bull regime (buffer 0) the rule is the same as before.
- **Owner call:** this cuts exposure, in line with the loss-minimization rule. If you want a different free-cash
  bar, set `system.slot_expand_free_cash` in config.
