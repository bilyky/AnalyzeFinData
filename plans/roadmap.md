## 🕯️ Candlestick & Pattern Library Expansion:

24. **High-Value Candlestick Pattern Expansion (`aether/signals.py`):** The engine currently nets **17** patterns (Engulfing, Harami +strict, Doji, Piercing/Dark Cloud, Star, Tasuki, Three Methods, Hikkake, Tweezers, On-Neck, Stick Sandwich + Kaabar's Bottle, Double Trouble, Quintuplets, Slingshot, H). Several high-reliability formations are **not yet implemented** and are worth adding — but **backtest-first, per the Jul-18 optimizer discipline**: add a detector to `aether/signals.py`, wire it into `patterns.candlestick_score()`, measure its 10-day forward-return spread via `backtest_ratings.py`, and only keep it if the spread is statistically meaningful (|z| &ge; 1.96, adequate N). Candidates, roughly by practitioner/Bulkowski reliability:
    *   *Three White Soldiers / Three Black Crows:* strong three-candle momentum reversals (Bulkowski ranks Three Black Crows ~78%).
    *   *Three Line Strike (Bull/Bear):* Bulkowski's highest *reversal rate* (~84%) — but note his own caveat below.
    *   *Three Inside Up/Down & Three Outside Up/Down:* the **confirmed** variants of Harami and Engulfing (third candle confirms) — Bulkowski rates these above the raw two-candle versions, so they may out-alpha the Harami/Engulfing already in the engine.
    *   *Hammer / Hanging Man* and *Shooting Star / Inverted Hammer:* the classic single-candle long-shadow reversals — conspicuously missing from the current set.
    *   *Marubozu:* full-body, no-wick conviction candle — a clean momentum-confirmation input (pairs well with the Bill Williams MFI "Green" bar work, Aug 1).
    *   *Abandoned Baby (Bull/Bear)* and *Kicker/Kicking:* rare but violent gap-driven island reversals — high reliability when they do fire.
    *   *Rising/Falling Window (gaps):* simplest continuation signal; a cheap, high-frequency factor.
    *   **Critical Bulkowski caveat (do not skip):** a high candle *reversal rate* is **not** the same as strong *post-breakout performance* — a pattern can reverse 84% of the time yet make little money afterward. The gate is the **forward-return spread in `backtest_ratings.py`**, never the textbook reversal percentage.

33. **Goal-Progress Surface — Consumer-First `goal_sentry` Wiring (R&D #33):** Surface goal progress ("X days left to the $20K doubling goal, need Y%/day") cheaply on the dashboard and daily email by pre-computing it once via `scripts/utils/goal_sentry.py` (which already writes `Data/active_targets.json`). Originated from a PR #6 review recommendation to "trigger `goal_sentry.py` *first* in the summary/watchdog flow so `active_targets.json` is always fresh." **Investigation finding (2026-08-17): that wiring is architecturally backwards and was deliberately NOT actioned**, for two verified reasons: (a) **no consumer exists** — `goal_sentry.py` is the *only* reference to `active_targets.json` in the codebase; it is the writer and only runs by hand, so nothing (web dashboard, `server.py`/`data_api.py`, daily email, `--summary`) reads it — scheduling the writer "first" refreshes an artifact nobody opens; (b) **"run first" inverts the data dependency** — `goal_sentry` *reads* `ai_portfolio_game.json` equity and live E\*TRADE account equity, so running it at the *start* of the flow computes targets off the *previous* run's equity (freshly timestamped but stale-valued); the broker round-trip is the real cost, not the tiny-JSON read. Corrected plan (consumer-first, trigger-last, backtest-not-required — pure plumbing):
    *   *Give it a consumer first:* add a `/api/targets` endpoint (serves `active_targets.json`) + a dashboard progress line and/or a "days-left / required-daily-return" block in the daily email report path. Until something reads it, do NOT schedule the writer.
    *   *Trigger at the END of the update pipeline*, after equity/close is settled for the day — never at the front — so targets reflect the fresh close. Reuse the existing `run_sentry()`; piggyback on equity already fetched in the flow rather than adding a second broker round-trip.
    *   *Treat the artifact as display-only cache* (it already is): a missing/failed `active_targets.json` must be non-fatal for the consumer.
    *   *Keep the E\*TRADE call non-interactive* (`etrade.get_tokens(..., allow_browser=False)` + fallback equity, both already present) and never let a sentry failure block the summary/watchdog.
    *   *Red-green tests:* the consumer (endpoint returns computed fields; missing-file path degrades cleanly) and the trigger ordering (sentry sees post-refresh equity, not stale).

    *   *The Over-Penalization of Strength:* Currently, the system applies a flat **`-2.0` penalty** to `industry_strength=Strong` and a **`-1.5` penalty** to `lt_trend=Strong` to avoid chasing overextended breakouts. However, in strong uptrends, these exact strengths are the engine of massive runs.
    *   *The Momentum Penalty Trap:* High `momentum_score` (z-score >= 1.5) is currently penalized because the system flags it as "overbought breakout chasing."
    *   *The Proposed Fix:* Research and design **Sector-Regime Conditional Penalties** in `aether/scoring.py`. When the overall market breadth is healthy or a specific sector exhibits volume-confirmed breakouts, the system will **dynamically waive or positive-weight** these industry strength and high-momentum parameters instead of applying defensive penalties, allowing AETHER to cleanly catch explosive leaders like `RDW` and `BXC`.
