## 🕯️ Candlestick & Pattern Library Expansion:

24. **High-Value Candlestick Pattern Expansion (`aether/signals.py`):** The engine currently nets **17** patterns (Engulfing, Harami +strict, Doji, Piercing/Dark Cloud, Star, Tasuki, Three Methods, Hikkake, Tweezers, On-Neck, Stick Sandwich + Kaabar's Bottle, Double Trouble, Quintuplets, Slingshot, H). Several high-reliability formations are **not yet implemented** and are worth adding — but **backtest-first, per the Jul-18 optimizer discipline**: add a detector to `aether/signals.py`, wire it into `patterns.candlestick_score()`, measure its 10-day forward-return spread via `backtest_ratings.py`, and only keep it if the spread is statistically meaningful (|z| &ge; 1.96, adequate N). Candidates, roughly by practitioner/Bulkowski reliability:
    *   *Three White Soldiers / Three Black Crows:* strong three-candle momentum reversals (Bulkowski ranks Three Black Crows ~78%).
    *   *Three Line Strike (Bull/Bear):* Bulkowski's highest *reversal rate* (~84%) — but note his own caveat below.
    *   *Three Inside Up/Down & Three Outside Up/Down:* the **confirmed** variants of Harami and Engulfing (third candle confirms) — Bulkowski rates these above the raw two-candle versions, so they may out-alpha the Harami/Engulfing already in the engine.
    *   *Hammer / Hanging Man* and *Shooting Star / Inverted Hammer:* the classic single-candle long-shadow reversals — conspicuously missing from the current set.
    *   *Marubozu:* full-body, no-wick conviction candle — a clean momentum-confirmation input (pairs well with the Bill Williams MFI "Green" bar work, Aug 1).
    *   *Abandoned Baby (Bull/Bear)* and *Kicker/Kicking:* rare but violent gap-driven island reversals — high reliability when they do fire.
    *   *Rising/Falling Window (gaps):* simplest continuation signal; a cheap, high-frequency factor.
    *   **Critical Bulkowski caveat (do not skip):** a high candle *reversal rate* is **not** the same as strong *post-breakout performance* — a pattern can reverse 84% of the time yet make little money afterward. The gate is the **forward-return spread in `backtest_ratings.py`**, never the textbook reversal percentage.
25. **Alpha-Weighted Candlestick Scoring (`patterns.candlestick_score()`):** Today every pattern contributes an **equal** &plusmn;1 to the net (`(bulls &minus; bears) / 5 &times; 2`). Replace the equal weights with **per-pattern coefficients derived from each pattern's measured 10-day forward-return spread** (same optimizer path as `scoring.py`/`patterns.py` weights, Jul-18), so a Doji (weak, ambiguous) no longer counts the same as a confirmed Three Outside Up. Keep each weight independently toggleable; default any un-backtested pattern to its current unit weight so the change is non-regressive.
26. **Option Trading & Volatility Patterns (R&D #26):** Research and design a quantitative modeling layer for option pricing, implied volatility (IV) rank/percentile, and options-based hedging/income strategies (e.g. covered calls, cash-secured puts, iron condors). Define high-reliability volatility-squeeze breakout patterns (like Bollinger Band + Keltner Channel squeezes) and backtest options delta-hedging heuristics head-to-head under our strict risk rules.
27. **Dynamic Momentum Rotation Engine (R&D #27):** Research and design a regime-adaptive slot-swapping mechanism inside AGGRESSIVE mode. Allows the portfolio to close out low-scoring, mature held positions (already in profit/breakeven) to dynamically deploy capital into high-scoring watchlist breakout leaders (combined score >= 12.0) when all 7 slots are full, capturing massive, time-sensitive momentum opportunities.
